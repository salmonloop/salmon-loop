## 2024-05-24 - Improve selection hint scannability
**Learning:** For complex command-line selection interfaces or overlays using Ink, verbose sentence-based navigation hints consume too much horizontal space and reduce scannability.
**Action:** Consistently use explicit, symbol-based microcopy (e.g., `↑↓ nav · space toggle · ↵ confirm · esc cancel`) for discoverability.
