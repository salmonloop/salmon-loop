## 2024-06-03 - Simplify CLI selection microcopy
**Learning:** For complex command-line selection interfaces or overlays using Ink, verbose sentence-based navigation hints consume excessive horizontal space and reduce scannability.
**Action:** Consistently use explicit, symbol-based microcopy (e.g., `↑↓ nav · space toggle · ↵ confirm · esc cancel`) for discoverability.
