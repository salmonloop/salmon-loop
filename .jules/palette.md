## 2025-06-09 - Use Symbol-Based Navigation Hints
**Learning:** Verbose sentence-based navigation hints in the CLI (e.g., "Use Up/Down to navigate...") consume valuable horizontal space and reduce scannability.
**Action:** Consistently use explicit, symbol-based microcopy (e.g., `↑↓ nav · space toggle · ⏎ confirm · esc cancel`) for discoverability in complex command-line selection interfaces.
